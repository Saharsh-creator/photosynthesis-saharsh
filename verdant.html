02 / Scientific breakdown

The Photosynthesis Equation

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light Energy
↓
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ Carbon Dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

Why It Matters

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. Plants use this sugar to support growth, while oxygen is released into the atmosphere. This process supports food chains and helps maintain Earth's atmosphere.
