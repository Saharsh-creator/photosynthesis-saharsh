03 / BIOLOGY KNOWLEDGE CENTER

EXPLORE THE SCIENCE
🌿

Chloroplasts

Organelles inside plant cells where photosynthesis occurs.

Chloroplasts contain thylakoids, grana, and stroma. They are specialized to capture light energy and support the reactions of photosynthesis.

☀️

Chlorophyll

The green pigment that absorbs light energy.

Chlorophyll is found in the thylakoid membranes. It absorbs mainly red and blue wavelengths of light and reflects much of the green light.

💨

Stomata

Tiny openings on leaves that allow gas exchange.

Stomata allow carbon dioxide to enter and oxygen and water vapor to leave. Guard cells regulate their opening and closing.

💧

Water Absorption

Roots absorb water from the surrounding soil.

Water travels through xylem tissue toward the leaves. It supplies electrons and hydrogen ions for the light-dependent reactions.

⚡

Light Reactions

Light energy is converted into chemical energy carriers.

These reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes. Water is split, oxygen is released, and ATP and NADPH are produced.

🧬

Calvin Cycle

Carbon dioxide is incorporated into organic molecules.

The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma. It uses ATP and NADPH to help convert fixed carbon into G3P, a molecule used to build sugars.
